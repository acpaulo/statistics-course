Biostatistics

From statistical concepts to biological interpretation. Understand the methods, explore the data, and interpret the results in a biological context.

Welcome

This learning space combines theoretical explanations, biological examples, visual demonstrations, short exercises and guided R analyses. The emphasis is on choosing appropriate methods and interpreting results—not memorising commands.

How to use these lessons: Work through the sections in order. Try the questions, complete hand calculations using your formula sheet, and run the provided R code. An internet connection is required to start browser-based R.
