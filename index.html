Does antibiotic exposure change bacterial gene expression?

A short example of a lesson page with explanations, questions and R code that runs directly in the browser.

1 · The biological question

A researcher asks whether antibiotic exposure changes the expression of a bacterial stress-response gene in E. coli.

Six independently grown cultures were measured in each group. Each value represents one biological replicate. These are illustrative teaching data, not real experimental measurements.

Remember: the independent culture is the experimental unit. Repeated qPCR measurements from the same culture would be technical replicates; they would not increase the biological sample size.

3 · Choose and run a statistical test

The groups consist of independent cultures. We want to compare their population means. The Welch two-sample t-test is a suitable starting point; it does not assume equal population variances.

Hypotheses
H0: the population mean expression is equal in the two groups.
H1: the population mean expression differs between the two groups.

4 · Effect size: how large is the difference?

A p-value does not measure the magnitude or biological importance of an effect. The raw mean difference is expressed in the original measurement units. Cohen's d expresses the difference in pooled standard-deviation units.
