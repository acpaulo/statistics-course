Biological replication and bacterial gene expression

A short student workbook combining theory, knowledge checks, R practice and written interpretation.

1. The biological question

A researcher asks whether antibiotic exposure changes expression of a bacterial stress-response gene in E. coli.

Six independently grown cultures are measured in each group. Each value represents one biological replicate. These are illustrative teaching data.

Key idea: the independently grown culture is the experimental unit. Repeated qPCR measurements from the same culture are technical replicates; they do not increase the number of biological replicates.

4. Statistical test and interpretation

The groups contain independent cultures. A Welch two-sample t-test compares the population means without assuming equal variances.

H₀: the population mean expression is equal in the two groups.
H₁: the population mean expression differs between the two groups.
